## Decisions

[#decisions](#decisions)


- Propose a Ownership + Contribution Model
    - Ownership/relationship model– soft proposed text:
        -   **Program-owned production project**
            Official deployment, named maintainers, clear release authority. Community PRs welcome, but maintainers decide merge/release.
        -   **Community-maintained library**
            Named community maintainers own day-to-day merges; program sets compatibility/security requirements and can approve major releases.
        -   **Incubator / reference implementation**
            Community-led experimentation. Faster iteration, clearly labeled non-production; graduation criteria define when it becomes program-owned.
        -   **Specification / shared standard asset**
            Changes require broader review and documented consensus, since multiple projects depend on it.
        -   **Unrelated** and **Acknowledged**
            Not in our orbit; either no-contact or aware-but-not-in-scope.
            
    - Governance framework:
        - macro (AWG) sets standards + escalation path
        - micro (project) owns day-to-day process and merge authority. Examples
            - Anne empowered to directly approve validation-library testing/CI PRs (quality/engineering level, not release-level).
            - Dave to accept/reject functionality / suggestions, so long as doesn't exceed establish footprint parameters.f
- AWG consensus: Jerry's CI/testing contributions are good and should be adopted.
  - Ownership assigned on PR review + change management (merges)
  - Expectations on timing/lifecycle
